# Business hypothesis — 8 November 2026

## Product and buyer

RetainerLens helps the owner of a small design, web or marketing agency review monthly retainer delivery margins without replacing their time tracker. Initial scope is a useful review calculator, followed by saved monthly history and comparisons if buyers validate that need. Working name only; domain and trademark clearance have not been completed. No legal entity has been formed.

## Evidence and uncertainty

Existing products establish a recurring workflow, but they do not establish demand for our product. Harvest offers profitability reporting in its higher-tier product; spreadsheets and competing standalone tools already cover parts of this problem. Differentiation must come from fast setup, a focused monthly decision workflow, and price. Direct buyer interviews and purchase intent are still missing.

Sources reviewed:
- https://www.getharvest.com/pricing — established time tracking and reporting competitor.
- https://support.getharvest.com/hc/en-us/articles/31447072608397-Information-about-pricing-plans — plan and profitability feature information.
- https://followincome.com/templates/retainer-tracker — free spreadsheet alternative.
- https://mysmartcopilot.com/tools/agency-client-profitability/ — direct competitive workflow.

Rejected as the first direction: generic invoice reminders. Crowded with bundled features and standalone competitors, plus email delivery and accounting integration complexity before the first sale.

## Required revenue model

The user explicitly requires subscription-based profit. Paid access must use recurring subscriptions; no lifetime deals or one-off product sales will count toward the goal. The free preview supports acquisition. Subscription launch requires reliable saved monthly reviews, comparisons, transparent renewal terms, cancellation and billing management. $29/month per studio is the initial price hypothesis, subject to buyer validation.

## Pricing and profit target

Use USD as a provisional planning currency because the goal did not specify one. Test $29/month per studio; do not represent it as validated pricing. Profit target is $1,000/month after payment fees, hosting, software, refunds and acquisition spend; report owner labor and taxes separately until the owner defines accounting treatment.

Illustrative economics, not actual results: 40 subscribers × $29 = $1,160 monthly revenue. Assume 4% payment cost plus $0.30 per subscriber, and $50 fixed hosting/software: contribution after these costs is $1,051.60. This excludes refunds, ads, labor and taxes; extra costs require more subscribers. Verify actual processor pricing and record actual expenses before claiming profit.

Actual verified revenue: $0. Paying subscribers: 0. Ad spend: $0. Operating costs have not been connected to a ledger. Profit target has not been achieved.

## Validation and iteration

1. Publish source to the requested new public GitHub repository with README.
2. Host the working preview and set up a real feedback/contact route using an owner-approved business identity.
3. Prepare 10 targeted interview invitations to agency owners; do not send without explicit messaging authorization. Ask about their last monthly review, actual hours spent, current tools, and willingness to pay for history/comparisons.
4. Run five observed product sessions. Track completion of a real review, input errors, and decisions users actually make. Do not count fictional examples as activation.
5. Seek three explicit paid pilot commitments before expanding beyond the focused workflow. No fabricated waitlist or customer testimonials.
6. Connect an owner-controlled merchant account and test checkout, refunds, subscription cancellation and fulfillment before accepting payments. Owner handles identity and bank verification.
7. Deliver saved monthly history and comparison if the interviews support it; retain a usable free preview as an acquisition entry point.
8. Review actual revenue, costs, activation, paid conversion, retention and reasons for cancellation. Improve the largest evidenced bottleneck each cycle.

Advertising requires the user's approval of the channel, creative, destination and spending cap before launch. No approval has been requested yet because the sales funnel is not ready.

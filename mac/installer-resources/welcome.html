RetainerLens for Mac

Desktop preview · Version 0.1.0

Review client fees, delivery costs and margins in a native Mac workspace. Open a RetainerLens account export or create a local review document.

This installer places RetainerLens in Applications. Reviews stay in your local files. Shared account sign-in, automatic sync and subscription access are still in development.

Developer preview: this installer has not been signed with a Developer ID or notarized by Apple. macOS may block installation. Customer-ready distribution is pending.

No background services or installation scripts are included. Requires macOS 14 or later and the processor architecture shown on the download page.
